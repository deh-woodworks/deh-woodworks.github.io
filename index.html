About Donnie

The man behind the shop

Donnie Hurst served as a police officer in Coffee County for about 20 years, with the Coffee County Sheriff’s Office, the Douglas Police Department, the Nicholls Police Department, and the Broxton Police Department. For seven of those years he was a K9 handler with the Sheriff’s Office.

His career in law enforcement ended when he was injured in the line of duty and medically retired. These days you’ll find him in his shop. DEH Woodworks started as a hobby, and it’s his way of sharing a real talent with the people around him.

He’s a humble man and a great father. He’s a friend to a few and friendly to all, and he cares about the Coffee County community he served for so long.

Hurst Shepherds

In memory of Bo

Many folks in Coffee County may know Donnie from Hurst Shepherds, the working-line German Shepherd program he ran here. Its sire was Bo, registered as Dark vom Saddleberg, an Austrian import who was also Donnie’s K9 partner on the job. The females were all certified working-line Shepherds.

Donnie closed Hurst Shepherds after Bo passed away years ago. Bo was like another child to him, and those years are a memory he holds close to his heart.
